## Does any lab explicitly accept high school students or say how prospective students should reach out?

### Takeaway
Only one lab page I found speaks to high school students directly. The Hunt Lab welcomes them for a meeting and lab tour but explicitly does **not** accept high school internship applications. The institutional route for high schoolers in School of Medicine labs is the Student Research Intern Program (SRIP), which requires students to be at least 16 with parental authorization. Animal labs must get parental consent and add any minor to the IACUC protocol.

### Cited Findings
- **Hunt Lab (A&N, epilepsy):** "If you are in high school and interested in our research, we would love to meet you, discuss science and even give you a tour of the lab. However, due to the nature of our work, we are not accepting internship applications from high school students." The same page recruits undergraduates and a lab technician; the 2023–24 timing suggests it may be out of date. — [Hunt lab Join page (Squarespace mirror)](https://huntlab.squarespace.com/join-the-lab); [roberthuntlab.org/join-the-lab](http://www.roberthuntlab.org/join-the-lab)
- **SRIP (Student Research Intern Program):** a volunteer, non-credit course that places high school students (minimum age 16, with parental authorization) under the direct supervision of a School of Medicine professor, with restrictions on high-risk duties such as radiation exposure. — [UCI OB/GYN Research Compliance SOP (Mar 2018)](https://www.obgyn.uci.edu/PDF/Research-Compliance-SOP-SRIP-Mar2018.pdf)
- **UCI Office of Research, volunteers in animal labs:** PIs must vet unaffiliated volunteers, including local high schoolers. Minors need written parental consent and a registration form, and must be listed on the IACUC protocol even if they only observe. — [UCI Office of Research](https://research.uci.edu/animal-care-and-use/policies-and-guidance/volunteers-in-animal-labs/)
- **Neurological Surgery** has a "High School, Undergraduate and Medical Student Education Opportunities" page that includes a clinical observership in neuroscience. This is a clinical department, so it is outside the core scope but relevant. — [UCI Neurosurgery](https://medschool.uci.edu/research/clinical-departments/neurological-surgery/education-training/neurological-surgery/hs-undergrad-med-opportunities)
- **CNLM Summer Institute in Neuroscience:** a 2022 project listing exists. Its eligibility (high school or undergraduate) was not confirmed. — [CNLM 2022 projects](https://cnlm.uci.edu/2022projects/)
- **UCI Health High School Volunteer Program:** clinical, not research; ages 16+; applications were listed as closed. — [UCI Health](https://www.ucihealth.org/volunteer/high-school-students)
- **Bio 199 directory:** UCI's undergraduate research directory lists faculty by lab. It is for UCI undergraduates but shows which labs take students. — [Bio199 list](https://apps.bio.uci.edu/bio199/faculty/list)

### Inferences
- A strong first email to most of these PIs would mention SRIP (age 16+, parental authorization). The student could also ask for a lab tour or a computational/data-analysis project, since the Hunt Lab shows that some labs welcome visits even when they decline formal high school interns.
- Human-subjects, imaging, and computational labs (Stark, Yassa, Chrastil, Lopour) may be more workable than mouse labs, which need IACUC protocol amendments for minors.
