Pages could not be opened directly. Every fact below comes from web-search result excerpts of the cited page URLs. Where an email is marked **"Official page (search excerpt)"**, the search engine reported that address as appearing on that official UCI page, but I could not open the page and check it myself. Before sending, the student should open each linked page and confirm the address. No email was guessed or constructed.
>
> Confidence key for emails:
> - **A** = official UCI page (lab site / faculty.uci.edu / mind.uci.edu / center page), reported by a search excerpt; two or more consistent UCI sources where noted
> - **B** = official UCI page, single search excerpt only
> - **C** = publication or third-party only (not recommended for outreach)
> - **None** = not publicly listed in anything I could find

## Q1. Who are the research-active faculty in UCI Neurology and UCI MIND, with lab, focus, email, and activity?

### Takeaway
I identified 21 UCI faculty who currently appear to be research-active in Neurology, UCI MIND/ADRC, or closely affiliated programs. Eight of them have a publicly listed email (or lab inbox) on an official UCI page. Two well-known former UCI neurology researchers (Steven Cramer and Aimee Pierce) have **left UCI**, and their old UCI pages are still online. The strongest outreach targets with posted contact details are Bota (neuro-oncology), Sajjadi (dementia imaging), Ezzati (neuroinformatics/AI), Flanagan (neural stem cells), La Spada (neurodegeneration genetics), Henchcliffe (Parkinson's, department chair), Corrada (90+ Study), Baram (epilepsy/early-life stress), and Thompson (Huntington's).

## Q2. Does any lab or program explicitly accept high school students, undergraduates, or volunteers?

### Takeaway
None of the Neurology or UCI MIND faculty pages I found explicitly say they accept **high school** students. Several labs explicitly take UCI **undergraduates** (Bota via BIO 199; Head Lab lists undergraduates and volunteers; Sajjadi lab includes undergraduates). For high schoolers, the realistic routes are UCI School of Medicine-wide programs, mainly SCRIP (open to high schoolers aged 16+) and the Summer Online Research Program.

### Cited Findings
- **Head Lab (UCI MIND/Pathology):** the lab-members page lists graduate students, undergraduates **and volunteers** working on Alzheimer's/Down syndrome research. — [Head Lab members](https://sites.mind.uci.edu/headlab/lab-members/)
- **Bota Lab:** invites UCI undergraduates to apply through the BIO 199 website. It lists undergraduate student researchers. Graduate students and postdocs are told to email dbota@hs.uci.edu. — [Bota Lab contact](https://faculty.sites.uci.edu/botalab/contact-us); [Research Group](https://faculty.sites.uci.edu/botalab/?p=57)
- **Sajjadi Lab:** the PI page describes a lab of undergraduates, graduate students and postdoctoral trainees. — [Sajjadi PI page](https://faculty.sites.uci.edu/sajjadilab/dr-sajjadi)
- **Baram Lab:** a "Join Us" page asks prospective members to email baramlab@uci.edu with a cover letter and CV. The excerpt does not say which career levels it accepts. — [Baram Lab Join Us](https://faculty.sites.uci.edu/baramlab/joinus)
- **UCI MIND undergraduate track:** UCI MIND runs an "Anteaters Against Alzheimer's" course for undergraduates. Undergraduate volunteers help with outreach and study recruitment, and one received a Chancellor's Award of Distinction. I found no high school eligibility. — [UCI MIND news](https://mind.uci.edu/undergraduate-research-volunteer-receives-chancellors-award-of-distinction/)
- **Neural Repair Lab undergraduate page:** it offers Bio199 credit and recruitment/data roles, but it is **stale**: PI Cramer is now at UCLA. — [Neural Repair Lab undergrad page](https://faculty.sites.uci.edu/neuralrepairlab/research/undergraduate-opportunities)
- **SCRIP (Scientific Clinical Research Internship Program), UCI SOM Office of Research:** a volunteer, non-credit program. Eligibility explicitly includes high school students aged 16 or older. — [SCRIP](https://medschool.uci.edu/research/office-research/policies-and-guidelines/student-research-internship-program)
- **UCI SOM Summer Online Research Program:** a three-week mentored program for high school students. It typically closes to applications by late January or early February. — [Summer Online Research Program](https://medschool.uci.edu/education/pre-med-programs/summer-online-research-program)
- **Other UCI high-school routes:**
  - UCI Neurological Surgery (a different department) has a page on high school, undergraduate and medical student opportunities — [Neurosurgery HS page](https://medschool.uci.edu/research/clinical-departments/neurological-surgery/education-training/neurological-surgery/hs-undergrad-med-opportunities)
  - UCI Cancer Research Institute Summer Youth Science Fellowship, for Orange County 11th-12th graders — [CRI HS](https://cancerresearch.uci.edu/education-and-training/high-school-students/)
  - PERI Institute STEM program, six weeks — [PERI](https://educationresearch.uci.edu/the-peri-institute-stem-education-research-program/)

### Inferences
- SCRIP is the most direct formal path for a 16+ high schooler into a School of Medicine clinical department such as Neurology. A faculty member willing to host would likely still be needed, so cold emails to the Q1 list and a SCRIP application work together. This is an inference; I did not see SCRIP's mentor-matching rules.
- The Head Lab is the only neurology-adjacent lab whose page explicitly mentions "volunteers," so it is a strong candidate. Its PI email was not found; the CFAR-DS inbox may work as a route.

### Gaps
- I could not open the SCRIP or Summer Online Research Program pages, so current deadlines, fees and 2027 dates are unconfirmed.
- I found no UCI MIND page explicitly offering high school volunteer positions.
