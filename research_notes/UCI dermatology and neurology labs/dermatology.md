## Who are the research-active faculty in UCI Dermatology (including joint and skin-science affiliates), and what do they study?

### Takeaway
I identified 16 UCI faculty who run skin-research labs or programs and have search-visible evidence. The core group is 7 people with Dermatology appointments: Kelly, Ganesan, Shiu, Mesinkovska, Grando, Zachary and J. Smith. The other 9 are skin-science faculty in Biological Chemistry, Developmental & Cell Biology (DCB) and Biomedical Engineering who belong to the "UCI Skin" NIH core center. Ganesan, Shiu, Mesinkovska, Plikus, Andersen and Dai show the clearest 2024-2026 activity. Grando and Zachary appear only on a possibly outdated department list.

### Cited Findings

**Department context**
- The UCI Dermatology faculty work as principal investigators in clinical, translational and basic research. Clinical trials run through the Dermatology Clinical Research Center. The department is tied to "UCI Skin", one of only six NIH-funded Skin Disease Research Core Centers, and runs an NIH training grant for physician-scientists. — [UCI SOM Dermatology Research](https://medschool.uci.edu/research/clinical-departments/dermatology/research)
- The chair is Kristen Kelly, MD. The department reports 46 faculty and 16 residents. — [Message from the Chair](https://medschool.uci.edu/research/clinical-departments/dermatology/about-us/message-from-the-chair); [About Us](https://medschool.uci.edu/research/clinical-departments/dermatology/about-us)
- The Interdisciplinary Skin Science Program "is comprised of over 20 highly interactive skin research faculty." — [UCI Health Affairs, Interdisciplinary Skin Science Program](https://healthaffairs.uci.edu/research/falling-leaves-foundation-medical-innovation-building/interdisciplinary-skin-science-program/)
- These pages likely hold the authoritative rosters, and the skincenter faculty page reportedly lists emails, but I could not open any of them: [Dermatology Faculty Research](https://medschool.uci.edu/research/clinical-departments/dermatology/research/faculty-research), [Dermatology Faculty](https://medschool.uci.edu/research/clinical-departments/dermatology/faculty), [Research Faculty and Staff (older site)](https://www.dermatology.uci.edu/research_faculty_staff.asp), [Basic Research (older site)](https://www.dermatology.uci.edu/basic_research.asp), [UCI Skin center faculty](https://skincenter.uci.edu/People/faculty.php), [UCI Skin leadership](https://skincenter.uci.edu/People/leadership.php)

### Inferences
- Best first-tier targets for a high school student are labs with large basic-science groups, active 2025-2026 output and their own websites. That points to Ganesan, Plikus, Andersen, Dai and Shiu. The Mesinkovska clinical research center may suit someone interested in patient-facing trials, but clinical research usually limits minors' involvement.
- Grando and Zachary should be lowest priority until their status is confirmed. They appear only on a page the search summary described as possibly dated (around 2018-2019).
- Ganesan, Shiu and Plikus are highly connected through co-authorship and shared centers. One well-written email to a lab manager or postdoc in one of these labs may get forwarded to the others.

## Does any lab page mention accepting high school students, undergraduates or volunteers, or give instructions for prospective students?

### Takeaway
I found no explicit statement from any UCI dermatology or skin lab about accepting high school students or volunteers. The only formal research-training pathway found is for UCI medical students.

### Cited Findings
- Course 699D (Departmental Research in Dermatology) is for UCI medical students in years 3-4. They do clinical and translational research at the Dermatology Clinical Research Center under its Director. It requires Associate Dean approval and a DocuSign application. The scheduling phone line is 714-456-8462. — [699D elective page](https://medschool.uci.edu/education/medical-education/medical-degree-program/curriculum/electives/dermatology-elective-699d)
- The Xing Dai lab site has a "Contact Us" page. I could not read whether it mentions prospective students. — [Xing Dai Lab Contact](https://faculty.sites.uci.edu/xdai/?p=582)

### Inferences
- The absence of a high school policy in search results doesn't mean labs refuse high schoolers. It means the student should email PIs directly and ask, or look at UCI-wide pre-college research programs. I did not research those here.
